# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static, two pages: `site/index.html` and `site/menu.html`, one shared stylesheet and one small script in `site/assets/`. Two self hosted font families. No build step, no dependencies. Chosen so the concept can be dropped on any static host (Cloudflare Pages, output directory `site`) or sent to the owner as one link.

## Users

- **The owner, Carmelo Savarino.** This is a pitch; he did not ask for a site. He sees it first on his phone and must say "that's us" within five seconds.
- **Columbia locals** deciding what to grab today: a sandwich, bread, a box of cookies, a family pan.
- **Day trippers from Nashville and visitors from New York and New Jersey** who know the family's earlier restaurant or the Nashville Scene pick, checking where it is and whether it is open.
- **Anyone ordering ahead:** cakes, cookie trays, catering, the family meal.
- **Restaurant buyers** looking for a wholesale bread and pastry source.

## Product Purpose

A two page concept for Savarino's Market, a Sicilian family bakery, deli and market at 314 West 11th Street, Columbia, TN 38401, 931-223-8335. It has to get people in the door (hours, menu, map, parking), take cake, cookie tray and catering orders, capture wholesale inquiries, and tell the family story briefly. Priorities in that order.

## Brand Commitments

- **The color logo is the brand.** Palette sampled from it: navy `#001C41` (text, dark ground), ivory `#FDFEF8` (light ground), teal `#069DA3` (rules, borders, tile work, buttons with navy text), saffron `#F1B21C` (the sparing accent), cannoli gold `#F2BB5D` (illustration only). Roughly 60 ivory, 30 navy, 10 teal and saffron. Never paragraphs in teal.
- **A continuation, never a rebrand.** The color logo evolves the mark on their register screen. Display serif like the SAVARINO'S wordmark, one wide geometric sans like the register screen. The cannoli is an engraving in both logos; any illustration must match that etched style or be left out.
- **The eight words, spelled exactly:** CATERING · DELI · PASTAS · SANDWICHES / PASTRIES · COOKIES · CAKES · BREAD. They are the family's own words for what they do. All eight stay prominent in the hero, the homepage blocks, the menu headings and the footer, and are never renamed or merged.
- **Also from the register screen:** "Since 2002", the address written out, and the phone number as a tap to call link.
- **Voice:** short sentences, warm, direct, a little Brooklyn. Say "Sicilian" proudly. No em dashes or hyphens as pauses. Banned: nestled, culinary journey, elevated, artisanal, authentic more than once. One wink allowed: "Biscotti. Not biscuits."
- **No scraped or stock photos.** Imagery ships as labeled placeholder frames that name the exact shot.
- No gradients, emoji, thick side stripes, Italian flag colors, checkered tablecloths, mustached chefs or script fonts.

## Friction the site must solve

Easy to miss and can look closed from the street. Parking is unclear. The shop is small, Saturdays are busy, seating is outdoors only. Hot food can run out before closing. People do not know they can order cakes, trays and catering ahead.

## Operating Context

- Mobile first: most visitors arrive from social, maps or search on a phone.
- Primary actions everywhere: call, directions, the menu. A sticky bottom bar carries all three on phones.
- Ordering ahead is by short inquiry form or by phone. The concept forms validate but send nothing until wired to the family inbox.
- The open or closed status is computed in Central time from the verified hours.

## Needs the owner (never invent, always mark CONFIRM WITH OWNER)

Prices, lead times, catering minimums and terms, accessibility details, daily specials and the case contents, holiday hours, parking specifics, sandwich ingredients beyond those supplied, family pan sizes, wholesale terms, real photos, real review text, press wording, where inquiries are routed. ## Product Principles

1. The owner recognizes himself first; every line should be something the family actually says or did.
2. Calling and finding the door are never more than a thumb away.
3. The eight words on the sign run the whole site.
4. Mark every unknown plainly. A clean placeholder beats a confident guess.
5. Whitespace is the luxury signal; group with space, hairlines and type.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body contrast at least 4.5:1. Tap targets at least 44px. Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Every control reachable by keyboard with a visible focus ring.
