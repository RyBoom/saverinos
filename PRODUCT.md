# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static single file: one self-contained `index.html` with inline CSS and JS, Google Fonts only. No build step. Chosen so the prototype can be dropped on any static host or sent to the owner as one link.

## Users

- **The owner, Carmelo Savarino.** This is a cold pitch; he did not ask for a site. He will see it first on his phone and must recognize his own business within five seconds.
- **Columbia locals** deciding what to grab today: a sandwich, bread, a box of cookies, a family pan.
- **Day-trippers from Nashville** who remember Savarino's Cucina in Hillsboro Village or read the Nashville Scene pick, checking where it is and whether it is open.
- **Restaurant buyers** looking for a wholesale bread and pastry source.

## Product Purpose

A homepage prototype for Savarino's, a family-run Sicilian-American bakery, deli and market at 314 W 11th St, Columbia, TN. It has to get a phone visitor to call, pre-order, or show up, and it has to make the owner say "that's us."

## Positioning

Three generations of one family's baking: Sicily, Brooklyn (Veniero's), Nashville (Nolensville Pike, Berry Hill, Savarino's Cucina), now a 19th-century brick building on 11th Street that used to be a jail, a hotel and an upholstery shop. The Sandwich Gang board and the Palermo are theirs alone.

## Operating Context

- Mobile-first: most visitors arrive from TikTok, Yelp or Maps on a phone.
- Ordering is by phone only. Primary action everywhere: tap to call (931) 223-8335. No online ordering, not even as a placeholder.
- Pre-orders for family pans and custom cakes need lead time; the lead time is not yet confirmed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Made from scratch: Italian bread and hoagie rolls, cannoli, sfogliatelle, bomboloni, biscotti, pignoli and rainbow cookies, tiramisu, custom cakes, fresh pasta and sauce, family-size pans (lasagna, stuffed shells), deli meats and cheeses, made-to-order sandwiches (the Palermo, the Sicilian, sausage and pepper, chicken parm). Wholesale to restaurants.
- Hours conflict across listings: show an "Hours" slot with a clearly marked placeholder, never invented times. Same for pre-order lead time.
- No prices, no invented menu items, no invented people.
- Only the Palermo has a verified namesake (attorney Rose Palermo, the one woman on the Sandwich Gang board). Other sandwiches get short voice lines about the sandwich itself, no invented namesakes, tagged "confirm with owner" where current availability is unsure.

## Brand Commitments

- Name lockup: "Savarino's" leads, with "Bakery, Deli & Market" as the descriptor.
- Voice is Corrado's: short, warm, funny, a little Brooklyn-blunt ("Biscotti. Not biscuits."). No corporate filler. No em dashes or hyphens as sentence pauses; use commas, colons or periods.
- No photos or logos from their website or social media. Imagery ships as designed placeholders that name the exact shot needed.

## Evidence on Hand

- Nashville Scene, Best of Nashville 2023: "Best Reason to Go to Columbia."
- Top-ranked Columbia bakery on Yelp.
- Featured on Diners, Drive-ins and Dives (Savarino's Cucina, Hillsboro Village, 2006 to 2017).
- Strong TikTok following (no follower count verified; do not state one).
- Timeline: bakery on Nolensville Pike (2002); wholesale bread from a Berry Hill house the family called "the dump"; Cucina 2006 to 2017; Columbia wholesale launched July 4, 2019; boxed biscotti sold through liquor stores during the pandemic; retail opened 2023.
- People: Corrado Savarino Sr. (founder, born in Sicily, Brooklyn from age 9, trained at Veniero's); Carmelo (owner); Corrado Jr. (younger brother, works alongside him). Dad still sits on the porch; the porch table is for family.
- Absent: photos, logo, hours, lead times, prices, testimonials. Do not fabricate.

## Product Principles

1. The owner recognizes himself first; every line should be something the family actually says or did.
2. Calling is the action. It is never more than a thumb away.
3. The Board is the signature: the sandwiches and their stories carry the brand.
4. Mark every unknown plainly. A clean placeholder beats a confident guess.
5. Funny beats fancy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body contrast at least 4.5:1. Tap targets at least 44px. Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
