Our Story

Sicily to Brooklyn to Nashville to 11th Street. One family, a lot of bread.

  1. Sicily

    Where it starts

    Corrado Savarino Sr. is born in Sicily.

  2. Brooklyn

    Age nine

    The family moves to Brooklyn. That’s where he grows up.

  3. Lower East Side

    Veniero’s

    He trains at Veniero’s in Manhattan. Yes, that Veniero’s.

  4. 2002

    Nolensville Pike, Nashville

    Savarino’s opens a bakery on Nolensville Pike.

  5. Berry Hill

    “The dump”

    Wholesale bread comes out of a house in Berry Hill. The family calls it “the dump.” The bread was great anyway.

  6. 2006 to 2017

    Savarino’s Cucina, Hillsboro Village

    Regulars get sandwiches named after them and the Sandwich Gang is born. Diners, Drive-ins and Dives stops by.

  7. 2019

    W 11th St and Parker St, Columbia

    Carmelo buys a 19th-century brick building on the corner and starts wholesale on July 4, 2019.

The building had a few lives before us.

The building at W 11th and Parker was a jail, then a hotel, then an upholstery shop. Now it’s Savarino’s.

Jail, hotel, upholstery shop. Now it’s Savarino’s. The only thing doing time here is the dough.

  1. The pandemic

    Biscotti, by other means

    We sell boxed biscotti through liquor stores. Don’t knock it. It kept the ovens on.

  2. 2023

    The front door opens

    Retail opens on 11th Street. Now anybody can walk in.

  3. Now

    Carmelo, Corrado Jr. and Dad

    Carmelo runs the place. His younger brother Corrado Jr. works right alongside him. Dad’s on the porch.
