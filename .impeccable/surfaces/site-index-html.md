# Homepage (site/index.html)

Scope: single-page homepage prototype, mobile-first. Mode: Persuade (brand register).
Audience: the owner first (cold pitch, phone, five seconds), then locals, Nashville day-trippers, restaurant buyers.
Action: tap to call (931) 223-8335. No online ordering.
Proof: Nashville Scene Best of Nashville 2023 "Best Reason to Go to Columbia"; top Columbia bakery on Yelp; Diners, Drive-ins and Dives (Cucina).
Constraints: hours and pre-order lead time are marked placeholders; photos are designed placeholders naming the shot; no invented people, prices or ingredients.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the building: a painted wall sign on 19th-century brick, not a food-photo bakery template with cream and script.
OWN-WORLD: White ground; brick-red wall fields; lamp-black painted menu board; ochre sign paint for calls and numbers. Condensed American gothic caps plus a Clarendon. Rules, not boxes. Placeholders are a sign painter's dotted pounce layouts.
STORY: Visitor sees it's Savarino's on 11th Street, that the sandwiches have names and history, and calls.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile: brick wall, SAVARINO'S full width in white gothic, descriptor, one-line offer, today's status slot, address, full-width ochre call button, all above the fold. Desktop: sign spans 7 columns; offer, status and call below in 3/2/2.
FORM: Painted sign on brick, grounded candidate 3 of 7, seed key ce11ea86 (degraded roll, no challengers).
