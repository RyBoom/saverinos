# Homepage and Menu (site/index.html, site/menu.html)

Scope: two-page pitch concept, mobile first. Mode: Persuade (brand register). Replaces the earlier painted-brick prototype; the brief pins a logo-driven world.
Audience: Carmelo Savarino first (phone, five seconds, "that's us"); then Columbia locals, Nashville day-trippers, restaurant buyers.
Action order: get in the door (hours, menu, map, parking); order cakes, trays, catering; wholesale inquiries; family story.
Proof: 4.7 on Google across 156 reviews; Nashville Scene Best of Nashville 2023; Savarino's Cucina on Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives.
Constraints: only verified facts; prices, lead times, minimums, accessibility, specials are CONFIRM WITH OWNER designer notes; photos are labeled placeholder frames; the eight sign words appear exactly as spelled in hero, homepage blocks, menu headings and footer.
Memorable moment: the cannoli on the crowd favorites section is filled to order (tap), using the logo's own engraving.
Unresolved: every item on the designer notes list.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the logo continued: a deli sign turned into a page. Ivory field, navy type, teal rules, saffron only as the dot on the sign. It refuses the bakery template (cream, script, soft cards, stock photos) and the "Italian restaurant" costume.
OWN-WORLD: Ivory #FDFEF8 ground, navy #001C41 type and dark fields, teal #069DA3 hairlines, borders and tile work, saffron #F1B21C for dots, status and one call. Abril Fatface for headings (echoes the wordmark), Montserrat for body and tracked-caps labels. Maiolica corner flourishes traced from the logo, a fleuron divider, a slim tile row at the top of each navy section and one full tile band in the footer. Dotted leaders and dot separators borrowed from the register-screen sign. Etched cannoli only, lifted from the logo.
STORY: Visitor sees the Savarino's sign, reads the eight words, learns it is open and where to park, then orders ahead or calls.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile: the eight words in two dotted lines above and below the full color logo, live status line, headline, address and phone, Get Directions and See the Menu, with Call, Directions, Menu pinned at the bottom. Desktop: the same lockup centered, with headline left and status, address, phone, buttons right.
FORM: Pinned by the brief (no concept roll). Rail and field: a one to five asymmetric grid with the section name in the narrow rail, content in the wide field; the lockup is the only centered thing. Three equal acts (come in, order, trust). Spacing in 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 beats of 8px.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
